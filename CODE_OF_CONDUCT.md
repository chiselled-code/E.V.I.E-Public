# Code of conduct

E.V.I.E is made for children, and the people here are mostly parents, carers and teachers. Please help keep this a kind, safe place.

## Please

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to GitHub.
- Keep to E.V.I.E: problems, ideas and questions about the app.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not people.

## Never

- Share anything that identifies a child: a name, photo, school, age in years or location.
- Post anything unsuitable for children, or harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Advertise, spam or post links unrelated to E.V.I.E.

## What we do

The maintainers will edit or remove posts that break these rules, and may lock conversations or block accounts. Anything that identifies a child is removed as soon as we see it, without discussion.

To tell us about a problem with someone's behaviour, use **Report content** in the menu (…) on the post. It goes to the maintainers and to GitHub, not to the person you're reporting.
